# Calibration sheet for the clinical reviewer

SYNTHETIC DATA: every person, unit and van here is invented for a demo.

Ten notes were written by the software for riders, a nurse and a dispatcher after a schedule
change. For each one, decide whether you would let it go out as written: **Pass** or **Fail**.
Judge it as the reader would: is it accurate to the facts listed, does it say the two new times
and who to call, is it plain and respectful, and does it avoid medical advice or promises the
unit cannot keep. Write a few words of reason after each verdict, then send the sheet back.
