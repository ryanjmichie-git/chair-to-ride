# Harborline Dialysis Unit — floor policy

We run three shifts a day on twelve chairs. Put-on windows are 06:00–06:45 for S1,
10:45–11:30 for S2 and 15:30–16:15 for S3. The unit closes at 21:00 and nothing
runs past that.

Staff can only put on four patients in any fifteen-minute step of a shift. That is
the stagger, and it is a staffing limit, not a preference. A schedule that starts
five people in the same quarter hour is not one we can work.

A chair needs thirty minutes of turnover between one patient coming off and the
next going on. That time is for cleaning and setup and cannot be shortened.

We never change a prescription. Treatment days stay MWF or TTS, and the session
length stays whatever the nephrologist wrote — 210, 225, 240, 255 or 270 minutes.
Sessions are never shortened, split, or pushed to a different day to make a ride fit.

Patients marked clinically fixed keep their start time exactly. The reason is on the
chart and the front desk does not overrule it.

Patients who have not consented to being moved keep their start time too. They can
still be given a better ride home; the chair simply does not move.

A patient with a hypotension note needs thirty-five minutes of recovery after the
session instead of the usual twenty. Site hemostasis, post vitals and weigh-out all
happen inside that window, and the ride should not be at the door before it ends.

We accept at most four chair-start changes per shift per day, and no single change
larger than thirty minutes. Beyond that the floor cannot absorb it.

Anything this policy does not allow goes to the charge nurse, not into the schedule.
